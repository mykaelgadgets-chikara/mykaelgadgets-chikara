# Building Chikara

I’m building **Chikara** — an AI-assisted operating system for Mykael Gadgets.

The goal is simple: turn everyday business work into reliable systems that help people move faster while keeping important decisions visible, reviewable, and human-controlled.

## What I’m working on

- Multi-agent coordination for real business operations
- Safer automation with confirmation, audit trails, and rollback paths
- Reliable workflows across operations, sales, finance, and customer care
- Turning daily lessons and failures into reusable product improvements

## How I build

I build from the work itself. Each useful improvement starts with a real operational need, becomes a controlled workflow, and earns trust through testing and evidence.

**Practical over flashy. Safe over rushed. Useful every day.**
